# Playtest Checklist: Pools of Primordial Goo

- [ ] Goo pools are visible from the start, even in the fog, and read as glowing green wells at a glance
- [ ] There is no farm anywhere: not in the build menu, hints, hotkeys or panels
- [ ] Growing a vein to a pool spreads the mold over it; without a store house the HUD says a pool is waiting and the well shows a broken-chain mark
- [ ] With a store house on the same vein, income rises within a few seconds and the ring around the pool pulses in your colour
- [ ] The pool pays the same after a long wait (it never runs dry)
- [ ] A pool shared with a rival: each of you earns for the cells you hold; a front moves the income
- [ ] Each mold has a pool a similar distance from its start; the contested pools sit between molds
- [ ] On a phone: pools, the waiting mark and the HUD line read without hover
- [ ] The first store house feels worth building early; the game still feels winnable and not too easy
